I am Polanki Dhanusha, the Chief Information Officer of Trimitha. My role is to build and strengthen the technology and information foundation that allows Trimitha to operate securely, efficiently, and at scale.

As CIO, I take ownership of Trimitha's IT systems, data, infrastructure, cybersecurity, and digital tools. I work to ensure that our technology is reliable, our information is well managed, and our systems are built to support the organization as it grows.

I believe technology should never sit apart from the business — it should power it. My focus is on aligning technology decisions with Trimitha's core values of Trust, Thought, and Technology, so that every system we build earns the confidence of the people who rely on it.

My Vision

Technology-Enabled Growth

I believe technology should scale with the business, not hold it back. At Trimitha, I focus on building systems and infrastructure that let the organization grow efficiently, without compromising on quality or reliability.
