Privacy Policy

At Trimitha, we believe that Trust is the foundation of every relationship. We are committed to protecting the privacy and security of our employees, clients, and visitors with absolute transparency. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information to ensure you feel secure in every interaction with us.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to all our users. This includes:

  • Personal Identification Information: Name, email address, phone number, and location when you voluntarily submit forms on our website.
  • Employee Data: For internal portal users, we store employment details such as Employee ID, role, department, salary, attendance records, and performance data securely in our database.
  • Usage Data: Information on how you interact with our website to improve user experience.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used strictly for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • To provide and maintain our services.
  • To manage employee payroll, attendance, and internal communications securely.
  • To respond to inquiries and support requests efficiently.

3. Data Protection

We implement industry-standard security measures, including Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) for employee access, secure database management, and encryption protocols to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at contact@trimitha.co.in.
